Receive a Crush archive from QR codes

Works with the animated QR stream (point the camera at the sender's screen) and with Crush paper backups (scan the pages with the camera, or open photos/scans of them). Codes can arrive in any order; missing ones are fine once enough have been read. The result is checked against its SHA-256 before it is offered. Nothing leaves this device.
