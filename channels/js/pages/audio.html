Send or receive a Crush archive as sound

The sender plays (or saves) a sound; the receiver listens with its microphone, or reads a recording. Frames that are drowned out are simply skipped: the transfer completes once enough frames have been heard, and the result is checked against its SHA-256. Nothing leaves this device.
